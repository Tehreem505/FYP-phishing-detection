What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers trick users into giving sensitive information like passwords, credit card details, or personal data by pretending to be a trusted source.

Types of Phishing

Email Phishing

Fake emails pretending to be from trusted companies.

URL Phishing

Fake websites that steal login credentials.

Smishing

Phishing attacks through SMS messages.

Vishing

Voice calls pretending to be official.

How to Identify Phishing

  • Check the sender address carefully
  • Hover over links before clicking
  • Look for urgency or threat language
  • Check spelling and grammar mistakes
  • Never share passwords via email
